1. Description of the service

TAGO is a mobile app for self-employed workers in Canada. It brings together a sales tax calculator for every province (GST, HST, QST, PST, RST) and deductions, time tracking by geographic zone, expense receipt management and invoicing (quotes and PDF invoices). There is no account to create, no TAGO server and no sync to TAGO (the backup copy to your own Google Drive is optional and encrypted): the app runs on your device, including offline. The data that leaves the device, and the limited cases where that happens, are described in the privacy policy.

2. Calculations: estimates to check before relying on them

The taxes, deductions, instalment payments, mileage and other amounts TAGO calculates are estimates. TAGO also helps you produce invoices with the mentions usually required by the Canada Revenue Agency and your province, and keep track of your expenses.

  • These features are provided as a help tool only. They are not accounting, tax or legal advice.
  • Rates, thresholds and calculation rules can change, and a result depends entirely on the information you enter.
  • You remain responsible for your filings and your invoices, and for checking the figures — with a professional or the tax authority concerned if needed — before using them for official purposes.
  • TAGO does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or compliance of the calculations and documents it produces.

3. How TAGO is checked

TAGO goes through repeated code reviews, including by artificial intelligence models, automated tests, error reports (Sentry, described in the privacy policy) and Google Play's pre-launch tests. These efforts reduce errors without eliminating them: they are not a guarantee.

4. Free tier and TAGO Pro

The whole app is free, including PDF invoices and email sending. The free tier keeps up to 10 client records, 25 documents (quotes, invoices, credit notes) and 25 expense receipts. TAGO Pro is a one-time purchase, managed and billed by Google Play, that lifts those three limits and adds the GPS trip measure and the trip offer when you drive. There is no subscription and no renewal. A purchase is restored from within the app on a new device or after a reinstall. Refund requests are governed by Google Play's policies.

5. Time tracking and location

Clocking in with the button does not read the device's location. Automatic zone entry and exit detection and GPS trip measuring use it, processed only on the device. Automatic detection is off by default, requires explicit consent before its first use and can be revoked at any time. You are responsible for the accuracy of your zones and time-tracking sessions, and for any manual correction you make to them.

6. Sending documents by email

Sending your documents (quotes, invoices, purchase orders, statements of account, annual statements) by email is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, it uses a Brevo API key you provide; the email goes out from your Brevo account, under that provider's terms. TAGO has no sending server and no visibility into these emails.
